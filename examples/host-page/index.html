Saturday, before the rush

A little room to wander

Pack a flask, leave the headphones behind, and follow the creek until the city sounds fade. The best route has no finish line.

We found early mushrooms under the alder trees and a bench with just enough sun for lunch. Nothing on the checklist needed doing.

In the bag

  • A notebook for leaves you cannot name.
  • Something warm to drink and something good to share.
  • A light jacket, even when the sky looks certain.
